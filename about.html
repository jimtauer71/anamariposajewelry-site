About me

Hi, I’m Ana

My name is Anamarie Reyes Kolden and I’m the founder of Ana Mariposa Jewelry, which is based in Minneapolis, Minnesota. I’m Mexican-American and have a long and personal history with jewelry. My ears were pierced when I was three months old, which is common in my culture. My family commemorated birthdays and special events with jewelry – from Mexican gold crosses for religious celebrations, a custom ring made by my uncle Joe instead of a quinceañera, pearl jewelry for graduations, jewelry that belonged to my mother and grandmother for milestone birthdays, and my wedding jewelry.

I began creating jewelry a couple of years ago as I transitioned from a long career as an attorney into the next chapter of my life. I have always created things with my hands – including stained glass work, knitting, cooking, and writing. I am happiest when I am creating anything from nothing – whether the nothing is a pile of vegetables, a skein of yarn, or beads, string, and wire.

My jewelry is inspired by my Mexican heritage, nature, and treasures from the earth and sea. I love the beauty of tumbled and faceted gemstones, the gleam of pearls, the shine of metal, the magic of seashells, and creating floral and other motifs with seed beads.

Mariposa means “butterfly” in Spanish. I have always loved Monarch butterflies, and I started noticing them everywhere after I left the legal profession. I believe the butterflies were signs pointing to my own metamorphosis from attorney to creator.
